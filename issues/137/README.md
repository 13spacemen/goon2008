### Comment 0: commenter 2, 2008-06-24 00:53 UTC

```
AI upload computer should have the 'cards' installed physically into it,
not &quot;uploaded&quot; like it is now. This has a few side effects:

 - The &quot;Reset&quot; upload command would no longer be required, as you'd just
have to uninstall the card itself
 - The cards, if used, will always be at the upload terminal and can't be
hidden away (making traitor's job easier)
 - The three initial laws can come built in and maybe eventually could be
removed with effort (maybe screwdriver/wirecutter/etc.)
 - Less clutter and storage space necessary to house a bunch of add-on
cards (as most would spend the entire round inside the computer)

This update would work well with issue 135 as well. 
http://code.google.com/p/ss13/issues/detail?id=135
```
