### Comment 0: commenter 24, 2008-08-07 00:08 UTC

```
Particularly in traitor mode, there is no current motivation for the
aggressive side to attack in any hurry. I'm thinking this could be
corrected by an absolute time limit (30 minutes / round of traitor), but
I'm sure we can do better.

So, what are some possible motivators that either encourage players to call
the shuttle or encourage the traitor to take action?Some quick thoughts...

- periodic solar flares (fry electronics through the ship)
- periodic random viral infections (lethal or just give genetic disorders)
- lack of sufficient fuel/oxygen/etc (current issue, but takes too long)
- traitor is infected with a fatal disease (cause disorder then death)

Even more useful would be conditions which provide a definitive end-state
avoiding current issues where the traitor dies early, but no one knows, and
avoiding the need for the escape shuttle.

- arrival of a &quot;rescue&quot; team which knows the identity of the traitor
- arrival of a &quot;transport&quot; team removing the item the traitor needs
- new shift of station workers (replace all members of the station)
- station repairs long range FTL communications/transport system
- station successfully meets research objective

I particularly like the last two since they rely on the station meeting
certain objectives and starts ensuring conflict between the traitor and
personnel.
```
