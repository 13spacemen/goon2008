Generally, non-shallow-scuba gas masks are designed to cover the eyes, ears, nose,
and mouth including any required glasses (though many manufacturers allow
custom/prescription inserts). We may consider making respirators (which typically

chemicals/contaminents (nerve agents, smoke, etc) in addition to just providing
'clean' air.
