### Comment 0: commenter 32, 2008-07-19 23:35 UTC

```
In Traitor the identity of the traitor should be found out by their actions
while carrying out their assigned objective, relying on the style of the
person playing. Shoving Centcom reports into that is more or less adding a
random chance to fuck the traitor right in the ass by broadcasting his
identity to the entire head of command, which usually leak the information
to the rest of the crew regardless.

I believe that two good alternatives are:

A system where there would be no report until the traitor uses their
syndicate radio, at which point SS13 communications intercept the signal
and generate a report listing the percentage chance to predict who sent the
signal and what item was requested, giving the traitor the choice between
an assisting item or complete anonymity.

Or just changing the report to notify head of command that there is a
traitor on the station, without any additional predictions to start the round.
```
