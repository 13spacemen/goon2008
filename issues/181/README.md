```
This is Space Cadet:

Basically what we discussed in the IRC chat earlier, and what I think would
work best:


1* In games with more than X number of people, create a certain Y% chance
based on the number of people more than X to have a crewmember that is not
the traitor be assigned the role of a sleeper agent.

2* It is important that while there might be a very high chance of having a
sleeper agent with a lot of people, don't make it guaranteed, so people
won't know for a fact that a sleeper agent is on board.

3* The sleeper agent was sent by The Syndicate as insurance, in case their
original agent fails.

3a* The sleeper agent has no uplink device, they are a fully embedded
crewmember.

4* The traitor knows nothing about any sleeper agent, period. They were
just sent on a mission to the station.

5* The sleeper agent knows the identity of the traitor. 

6* The sleeper can become active when they think the traitor is no longer
capable of doing their job, either through capture, incapacitation, or
death, or even just inaction after Z minutes. The sleeper will be informed
if the traitor receives a &quot;time of death&quot; message.

7* If the sleeper becomes active, they take on the objective of the old
traitor, in addition to a second (optional?) objective of killing the old
traitor.
If the traitor is captured alive (rarely), and the crew will decide to hold
the traitor to draw out the sleeper, the sleeper should still be more
focused on their main objective. If the objective to kill the first traitor
is optional, I think it will make more sense, ie: the sleeper doesn't want
the traitor to be analyzed, or whatever, but the main objective is
infinitely more important.

8* No suicide command for traitor/sleeper? Or maybe make some way for crew
to prevent a crew member's suicide?

9* While the traitor is active and the sleeper isn't, the sleeper is
basically a normal player by all means. Killing, sabotage, etc, before
becoming active is griefing.

10* This is a tough one: what if the traitor is succeeding? Then the
sleeper is to not purposefully impede the traitor's progress, but I
wouldn't say the sleeper should have to escape on the shuttle as well,
since even after the mission he will still be embedded. On the other hand,
the traitor will NOT fail his mission if the sleeper escapes along with him.

11* Freaking awesome idea to not overlook: AI as the sleeper. If the
original traitor's objective was to steal something and escape from the
shuttle, or something else that only a moving human could do, then provide
a &quot;new&quot; objective for the AI, since it's no longer possible. The playing
field has changed.

12* If the AI is the traitor and a player is a sleeper, then the same rules
apply. If after a certain time the AI doesn't fulfill its goal, then the
player takes over.

13* After 10 minutes or so, how about some way for letting the sleeper
agent know the traitor who he is, if he so choses? This may eliminate a
traitor accidentally killing the sleeper late in the round when all hell
can break loose.




 
```
