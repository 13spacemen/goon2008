```
Which brings me to my second suggestion, construction and deconstruction,
presently a rather neat aspect of the game. It has some gaps, though - it
would be great to shoot for every emplaced object being theoretically
constructible, but at the very least, it should be possible to build doors.
It could be quite simple - a pane of glass makes a regular door, glass (or
reinforced glass) plus wires makes a secure door, metal sheets plus wires
makes an airlock.

The disassembly chains could also do with textual feedback about your
process - this is most glaring when diassembling reinforced doors, when you
have to drag off the metal rods to really see the subtle visual feedback. A
simple 'done' in the textbox when you finish a step would be a big improvement.
```
