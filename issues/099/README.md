### Comment 0: commenter 1, 2008-06-02 23:39 UTC

```
One of the major reasons why everyone in SS13 adopts a &quot;toolbox first, ask
questions later&quot; policy is because, as a traitor, that's the only way to
take someone out before they can report it on their headset. Making it take
a while to say things on your headset, even though it would make traitoring
easier, would also add a ton of interesting gamplay options and stuff, like
actually letting people know you're the traitor before you kill them.

Ideally, you would say something out loud and then it would be transmitted
an amount of time later proportional to the length of the message, but only
if you're still conscious and have your headset on and so forth.

A traitor radio jammer item would somewhat fix this problem, but since
they'd have to give up another useful item for it, not entirely.
```
