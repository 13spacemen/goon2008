### Comment 2: commenter 25, 2008-07-17 19:13 UTC

```
I like this idea a lot.

Maybe even incorporate this into normal traitor mode! Have the job choice to spawn as
a stranded crewman.

Make the broken station have its own power grid, and a VERY limited supply of fuel to
the areas not exposed to space.

Make the oxygen in the sealed area depend on the power (filtration of some sort?), so
when the power goes out, the filtration fails, and the crew eventually has to switch
over to 02 tanks <<which must be manually filled from the 02 tanks in the broken
station, they should start out empty>>.
The kicker is that these 02 tanks are part of the filtration system, so the crew must
manage what remaining power/oxygen they have left.

Basically: make the system depend on filtration, so when the power goes out, nothing
is extracting CO2, plasma, etc, so some unfixable "leak" eventually floods the
outpost with poisonous gas (or somehow makes the atmosphere disappear through an
unfixable hole).

Also make deconstruction/construction of pieces of this tiny outpost important.

To prevent people from building a path out of floor tiles to the home station, make
the two stations somewhat far apart.

The broken station should start out with one space suit for EVA with NO jetpack (but
can refill one of the few 02 tanks to use)

**Jobs on the broken outpost**
Since power and oxygen will be actively conserved, most everyone of the tiny
"surviving" crew should mostly become de facto engineers/technicians of some sort.

Somebody will have to maintain the power if they plan on waiting out a rescue.

One person can use the EVA if he sacrifices some of the outpost's oxygen to refill an
oxygen tank.

Maybe have a surviving datacard filled with "research", possibly some cool genetics
instructions sequence (of the kind you can make using the green DNA machine) that can
be used on the main station's blue DNA machine. (The outpost's genetic machine should
be damaged/destroyed/offline of course!)
-An option would be to make this datacard be in a hard-to-reach place.

All I have for now on ideas.

```
