### Comment 0: commenter 25, 2008-07-11 19:28 UTC

```
Sometimes people don't make it to cryo.
In fact they usually don't, since most people tend to ignore the wounded,
and doctors aren't always the brightest.

When a person suffers cardiac arrest they have about a 3+-1 minute window
of having their heart restarted and surviving.

I propose having one or two defibrillators on the station, to slightly
bring back people whose hearts stopped from non-severe injuries.

A defibrillator can take a certain amount of time to deploy, charge, and
use, so you can't go around killing people with it.

Getting your organs destroyed obviously means you won't be coming back, so
how about a boolean that measures if it happens, and if not, then they have
 a chance of getting up to -80% or something damage, just long enough to go
into cryo. 

This will be far from a lifesaver, but it can give some deaths a fighting
chance.

In real life it's a 3+- 1 minute window, but in SS13, how about reducing
that to 1 minute total, since everything moves faster.

Then, it can take a bit to charge and use, and only have a chance of
bringing a person &quot;back&quot;.


What injuries leading to death do people sustain on the station that could
qualify for defibrillation?
```
