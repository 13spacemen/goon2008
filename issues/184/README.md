As we talked about in chat:

Normal and Electrical Toolboxes are very inconspicuous to carry around, the
best weapon in the game. 

Whoever isn't really paranoid if someone is standing next to them with a
toolbox usually winds up on the ground soon after.

Solution:

Something along the lines crowbars very good, and introduce lead pipes as

pipe section.

Toolboxes could be used as a coup de grace weapon? How about making it so

really hurts them. Think of it like a reverse-CPR.
